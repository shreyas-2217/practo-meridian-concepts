Concept 1

Practo for Animals

User job: Find trusted animal-care providers fast, especially in urgent moments.

  • Search-first hero with service + location inputs.
  • Custom SVG illustration with floating provider cards.
  • Category chips and dual-action provider cards.
  • Emergency strip with direct ambulance request.
  • Provider listing and correction CTAs.

Concept 2

Meridian AI Academy

User job: Find a focused AI skills programme that leads directly to employability.

  • Outcome-focused hero with bento stat grid.
  • Track switcher with curriculum cards tied to learner goals.
  • Transparent 5-step lead-capture journey.
  • Short registration form with instant success feedback.
24M+students learning online annually

AI Content Engine

User job: Turn raw ideas into polished Instagram/LinkedIn carousels without hiring a designer.

  • Numbered process diagram with tool-stack tags.

  • Human review gate before every publish.
