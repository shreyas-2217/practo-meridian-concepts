End-to-end workflow

Connected tools with human review right before publishing.

1

Capture the raw idea

Founder drops a topic, audience and key message into a Notion form, Airtable base or voice note. A structured prompt keeps inputs consistent.

NotionAirtableTypeform
2

Research & structure

An AI agent sources facts, summarises credible references and proposes a slide-by-slide outline: hook, body slides and CTA.

ChatGPT / ClaudePerplexity APIMake / n8n
3

Generate copy

Every slide gets a ≤6-word headline, 1–2 lines of body copy, plus caption and hashtags for the post.

ClaudeGPT-4oBrand voice doc
4

Create visual direction

AI builds on-brand image prompts and icon directions using the product palette. No stock-searching.

MidjourneyDALL·E 3Canva Brand Kit
5

Produce editable carousel

Canva Bulk Create or the Canva API fills a locked template with copy and visuals, exporting PNGs + PDF.

6

Review, edit & publish

Founder tweaks anything, then exports and schedules. Optional Buffer/Later integration queues posts.

Sample carousel output

Topic: "5 signs your pet needs a vet visit" — created for the Practo for Animals audience.

Slide-by-slide copy

  1. Hook: "5 signs your pet needs a vet visit"
  2. Slide 1: Sudden loss of appetite for >24 hours
  3. Slide 2: Unusual lethargy or hiding
  4. Slide 3: Repeated vomiting or diarrhoea
  5. Slide 4: Difficulty breathing or wheezing
  6. Slide 5: Limping or refusing to put weight on a leg
  7. CTA: "Find a trusted vet near you"

Full 1080×1080 7-slide carousel exported as PNGs from Canva. Icons generated with AI; typography and colours locked to the brand kit.
